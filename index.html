What is Rosalin?

Rosalin is a period tracker and fitness app. Track your menstrual cycle, get workouts and nutrition tips, and reach your daily health goals. Available in Arabic and English.

  • Menstrual cycle tracking and predictions
  • Workouts and fitness plans
  • Nutrition and wellness guidance
